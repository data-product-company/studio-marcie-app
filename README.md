# About marcie.app

**Do more of what you love: write!**

The MARCIE platform is for writers who are, or aspire to be, professionals. We help you build a stronger brand, increase your discoverability, and streamline the business side of your writing career. Our mission is to lighten the mental load that comes with being a professional writer so you can focus on your craft.

The studio provides tools for secure document management, content analysis, and secure file sharing. For those who would like to use it, the studio also offers AI-assisted brainstorming.

The primary goal of this application is to lighten the mental load on authors by providing a suite of tools to streamline their workflow, allowing them to focus on their craft.

## Your Public Brand: www.marcie.app

At **www.marcie.app**, we help establish your author brand within an "authors support authors" model. This is your public-facing home to connect with the writing community, share your journey, and build the brand that reflects your unique voice and work.

## Your Private Workspace: studio.marcie.app

*   **AI-Powered Brainstorming:** Collaborate with an AI partner that understands the nuances of storytelling, market trends, and author branding. Get support with everything from refining your prose to developing your next big idea.

*   **Secure File Sharing:** Share your manuscripts and other documents with confidence. Our platform allows you to send files with secure, temporary links that expire after a time you specify. You can even see when your shared file has been accessed.

*   **Your Second Brain for Creatives:** Never lose a fleeting thought again. Send your creative ideas, snippets of dialogue, or plot points directly to your private catalog via SMS. It's your personal, always-on "second brain" for capturing inspiration the moment it strikes.

*   **Centralized Document Hub:** Your private catalog for all your ideas and works-in-progress. You can upload larger files directly or even email them from your inbox for maximum convenience.

*   **A Commitment to Privacy:** Your ideas are your ideas. We have a strict, "anti-social" approach to your data. We will never sell your data or use your ideas to generate stories for others. We don’t want to write like you; we want *you* to write like you. For more details, please see our Privacy Policy.

Your workspace at **studio.marcie.app** is your post-login home. It is secure, private-by-design, and completely invisible to search engines and other web crawlers.

## Your Discovery Platform: books.marcie.app

To further our mission of driving discovery, we are proud to introduce **MARCIE Books** (`books.marcie.app`).

MARCIE Books is a public-facing discovery platform specifically designed to showcase the work of experienced authors with a backlist. It serves as a curated catalog where readers, agents, and publishers can explore and connect with talented writers who have a proven body of work. By highlighting your entire portfolio, MARCIE Books helps you gain the commercial visibility you deserve.

## Who is MARCIE.app for?

Our platform is for anyone who is serious about the craft and business of writing. Whether you are an established author with a backlist or an aspiring professional, MARCIE.app is built to help you develop a stronger author brand, increase your discoverability, and brainstorm new ideas with a knowledgeable partner.
